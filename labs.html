Labs

Each lab is a practical you might do in school, run here as a virtual experiment. You follow the method, collect your own data, process it, and then judge how good the result is. Every lab ends with questions in the style of Paper 1B, which tests experimental work.

How a lab works

  1. Plan

    Read the aim, the safety notes and the method. Decide which variables you change, measure and keep the same.

  2. Do

    Run the virtual experiment. Your readings change a little each time, just like real measurements.

  3. Process

    Turn your raw data into a result. Keep the units and give a sensible number of significant figures.

  4. Evaluate

    Work out the uncertainty, name the main sources of error, and suggest a realistic improvement.

A virtual lab does not replace real practical work. Use it to prepare for a practical, or to revise the method and the data processing before an exam.
