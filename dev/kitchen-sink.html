Structure 1.1.1

Elements, compounds and mixtures

An element contains only one type of atom. A compound contains atoms of different elements bonded in a fixed ratio, such as H2O or CuSO4.5H2O. A mixture contains more than one element or compound, in no fixed ratio, and they are not chemically bonded to each other.

A compound has different properties from the elements in it. A mixture keeps the properties of its parts, so it can be separated by physical methods.

“Pure” in everyday speech means natural or clean. In chemistry a pure substance is a single element or a single compound. Pure orange juice is a mixture.

You do not need to know about colloids, suspensions or alloys as separate categories of mixture here. Homogeneous and heterogeneous are enough.

Structure 1.1.3

Temperature and the kelvin scale

Temperature measures the average kinetic energy of the particles. The kelvin scale starts at absolute zero, so a temperature in kelvin is directly proportional to that average kinetic energy.

T(K) = T(°C) + 273.15
T(K)
temperature on the kelvin scale K
T(°C)
temperature on the Celsius scale °C

The conversion is not printed as a formula, but the data booklet gives STP as 273.15 K and 100 kPa. That tells you 0 °C = 273.15 K.

Write temperatures in kelvin as 298 K, with no degree sign. In gas calculations, always convert to kelvin before you substitute.

Converting a boiling point to kelvin

Liquid oxygen boils at −182.96 °C. Give this temperature in kelvin, to an appropriate number of decimal places.

  1. Starting from the formula shows the examiner your method.

    T(K) = T(°C) + 273.15

  2. The Celsius value is negative. Losing the minus sign is the most common slip.

    T = (−182.96) + 273.15 = 90.19 K

  3. When you add, the answer keeps the smallest number of decimal places in the data. Both values have two decimal places, so keep two.

    T = 90.19 K

90.19 K

Normal human body temperature is about 37 °C. What is this in kelvin? Give your answer to 3 significant figures.

  1. Which is bigger, the number in kelvin or the number in °C? Decide before you calculate.
  2. Add 273.15 to the Celsius value: 37 + 273.15.
T = 37 + 273.15 = 310.15 K

To 3 significant figures: 310 K.
