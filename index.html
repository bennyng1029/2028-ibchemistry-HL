How to study with this site

  1. Learn

    Read one short section. Stop at each key idea box and say it back in your own words.

  2. Watch

    Open a worked example. Try the next step yourself before you reveal it.

  3. Try

    Do the “Try it” calculation on paper. Use the hints only when you are stuck.

  4. Quiz

    Answer the quick check at the end of the section. Wrong answers are saved for later.

  5. Challenge

    Go to Practice for exam-style and challenge questions across all five topics.

Two labels to know

SL and Additional HL

Everything unmarked is standard level core. HL students need all of it. Content only HL students need is marked like this:

This block is Additional HL. It always has a violet edge and badge. If you are revising SL core only, use the switch in the header to hide every block like this one.

Not needed for IB

Chemistry goes deeper than your exam does. When a textbook, video or teacher mentions something beyond the syllabus, we tell you so you can stop there.
