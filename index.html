IB Chemistry · Structure 1, 2 and 3

What matter is made of, and how it holds together

Half of IB Chemistry is the Structure theme: what particles are, how they bond, and how we sort substances into families. This site takes you through it one short topic at a time, with every calculation worked through and plenty of questions to check yourself.

The topics

Three strands, eleven topics. Work through each strand in order.

Structure 1Models of the particulate nature of matter

Matter is made of particles. Learn how to describe them, count them and predict how they behave.

  • 1.1 Introduction to the particulate nature of matter
  • 1.2 The nuclear atom
  • 1.3 Electron configurations
  • 1.4 Counting particles by mass: the mole
  • 1.5 Ideal gases

Structure 2Models of bonding and structure

How particles hold together, and how the type of bonding explains the properties of a substance.

  • 2.1 The ionic model
  • 2.2 The covalent model
  • 2.3 The metallic model
  • 2.4 From models to materials

Structure 3Classification of matter

Sorting elements and compounds into families, so that you can predict what they will do.

  • 3.1 The periodic table: classification of elements
  • 3.2 Functional groups: classification of organic compounds

How to study with this site

  1. Learn

    Read one short section. Stop at each key idea box and say it back in your own words.

  2. Watch

    Open a worked example. Try the next step yourself before you reveal it.

  3. Try

    Do the “Try it” calculation on paper. Use the hints only when you are stuck.

  4. Quiz

    Answer the quick check at the end of the section. Wrong answers are saved for later.

  5. Challenge

    Go to Practice for exam-style and challenge questions across all five topics.

Two labels to know

SL and Additional HL

Everything unmarked is standard level core. HL students need all of it. Content only HL students need is marked like this:

Not needed for IB
