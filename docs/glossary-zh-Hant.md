### Three corrections to earlier project notes

1. **週期表, not 周期表.** Both E and C write 週期表, 週期, 週期性. 周期表 is the Mainland form (and the
   Japanese one). Use 週 everywhere.
2. **subshell / sublevel is 亞層**, not 支殼層. E gives 亞層，副殼層. 支殼層 is not in E or C.
3. **線光譜** (E) rather than 線狀光譜 for "line spectrum".

## 1. Style guide

### Register

- Plain standard written Chinese (書面語), the way a good Hong Kong secondary-school tutor writes notes.
  Short sentences. One idea in each sentence. Common words.
- Not Cantonese colloquial writing: no 嘅、咗、唔、係（＝是）、喺、啲、睇、搵、冇、俾、而家.
  Also avoid Cantonese-flavoured written forms: write 用自己的話 (not 用自己的說話), 停下來 (not 停一停),
  做得到 (not 做到), 這裏 / 那裏 are fine.
- Not Mainland wording: 資訊 / 資料 (not 信息), 影片 (not 視頻), 預設 (not 默認), 登入 (not 登錄),
  按 / 點選 (not 點擊), 透過 (not 通過 for "by means of"), 素質 / 質素 (not 質量 for "quality":
  **質量 means mass**), 互聯網 / 網絡 (not 網路).
- Not Taiwan wording: 數據 (not 資料 for "data"), 網絡 (not 網路), 軟件 / 硬件 (not 軟體 / 硬體),
  程式, 儲存, 列印, 瀏覽器, 連結 are shared. For chemistry, see the "avoid" notes in each table.
- Hong Kong characters: 裏 (not 裡), 着 (for 看着、接着; 著 only in 著名、顯著), 甚麼 (not 什麼),
  線 (not 綫), 污 (not 汚), 衞 or 衛 (either, be consistent: use 衛), 台 for 平台.
- Warm but not chatty. No exclamation marks unless the English has one. No 喔、啦、吧、哦.

### How to address the reader

- 「你」, never 「您」, 「同學們」 or 「大家」.
- The site calls itself 「本網站」. Avoid 「我們」 unless the English says "we".
- Instructions: a plain imperative is fine (「先在紙上試做。」). Use 「請」 for error and form messages
  (「請先輸入答案。」). Do not stack 請 in every sentence.
- Praise is short and factual: 「全部答對。」「做得好。」 Feedback on a wrong answer never blames:
  「仍未正確」, 「再試一次」.

### Numerals and units

- Half-width Arabic digits for every quantity, count, step number and year: 3 個步驟, 第 2 題, 2025 年.
  Chinese numerals only in fixed words (一次、兩種、第一電離能、三鍵、二氧化碳).
  Use 兩 (not 二) before a measure word: 兩個電子, 兩種同位素.
- Decimal point is a full stop: 6.02 × 10²³. Never a comma. Group digits the way the English does.
- Units are never translated and stay half-width: 25.0 cm³, 0.100 mol dm⁻³, 298 K, 100 kPa, 25 °C.
  One half-width space between number and unit (as in the English source). No space before % : 75%.
- Unit names in a sentence, when the English spells them out: 摩爾 (mol), 開爾文 (K), 帕斯卡 (Pa),
  焦耳 (J), 立方分米 (dm³), 立方厘米 (cm³), 克 (g), 千克 (kg). HK uses 厘米 / 分米 / 千克
  (not 公分 / 公寸 / 公斤 in science).
- Write "3 significant figures" as 3 位有效數字; "2 decimal places" as 小數點後 2 位.
- Ranges and ratios keep half-width signs: 1:2, 4s → 3d, 2–3 分.

### Punctuation

- Full-width in running Chinese text: ，。、；：？！（）「」『』. Quotation marks are 「」, with 『』 inside.
  Never “ ” in Chinese text.
- Half-width inside and next to numbers, units, formulas, placeholders and code: pV = nRT, (aq), 2.5e-2.
- Brackets: full-width （） around Chinese text or a mixed aside; half-width () for the English term
  after a key term, for state symbols, for oxidation states in names, and around a bare number or
  placeholder in a button, for example 「下一個提示 ({n} / {total})」.
- A list of items uses 、; clauses use ，. Use 和 / 及 / 以及 before the last item, without a comma.
- One half-width space between Chinese characters and Latin letters or digits: IB 化學, 第 3 題, 是 SL 內容.
  No space next to full-width punctuation: 例如 NaCl、MgO。
- The middle dot in headings such as "Structure 1, 2 and 3 · all topics" stays " · ".
- No italics for emphasis and no bold for whole sentences. Use `<strong>` on the one phrase that matters.

### Chemical names

- **Formulas, symbols, state symbols and equations are never translated.**
- IB uses IUPAC names. Translate the IUPAC name part by part, the E way:
  number-locants stay as digits with hyphens (丙-1-醇, 丁-2-烯, 2-甲基丙烷, 1,2-二氯乙烷).
- Use the systematic name, not the traditional one: 乙酸 (not 醋酸), 乙醇 (not 酒精), 甲醛, 丙酮, 乙烯, 乙炔
  (not 電石氣). If the English gives both, give both.
- Oxidation state in a name goes after the element it belongs to, half-width, no space:
  氧化鐵(III), 硫酸銅(II), 氯化鐵(II), 氧化銅(I).
- Oxyanions follow IB (sulfate, nitrate): 硫酸鹽 / 硫酸根離子, 硝酸鹽 / 硝酸根離子. E (2007) writes
  硫(VI)酸鹽 in the older Stock style. Do **not** copy that; IB does not use it.
- Ions: 鈉離子, 氯離子 (chloride ion), 氧離子 (oxide ion), 硫酸根離子, 銨離子, 氫氧離子 (hydroxide ion; C and
  HK textbooks say 氫氧離子, Mainland says 氫氧根離子).
- Isotopes: 碳-12, 氯-35 (half-width hyphen and digits).
- Element names: section 12. Use 矽 for Si.
- Acids: 氫氯酸 (hydrochloric acid; E and HK usage. 鹽酸 is the everyday name and is acceptable only if the
  English says "muriatic" or similar, which it never does), 硫酸, 硝酸.

### English alongside (the exam is in English)

- **Key terms** (`<dfn>` in the English): Chinese term, a space, then the English in half-width brackets
  inside the helper span: `<dfn>摩爾 <span class="en-term">(mole)</span></dfn>`. Only at the definition.
  After that, Chinese alone.
- **Command terms** in a question stem, first time in the question: 解釋 (explain), 推斷 (deduce).
- **Exam-wording callouts, markscheme points and model answers**: the site shows the English and puts the
  translation underneath. Translate plainly and completely. Do not add, remove or merge marking points.
  Do not "improve" the English wording in the translation: if the English says "electrostatic attraction
  between oppositely charged ions", the Chinese must name the same three things.
- **Never translated:** SL, HL, AHL, IB, Paper 1A / 1B / 2, Structure 1.4.2, the theme names
  "Structure" and "Reactivity", the site name, pV = nRT, variable letters, Rf, VSEPR, IR, NMR, TMS, STP.
- A phrase that earns the mark in English (for example "sea of delocalized electrons") may be quoted in
  English inside 「」 after the Chinese when the English text draws attention to the wording.

### IB versus HKDSE: keep IB, add a short note

Her Hong Kong friends learn some things differently. Keep the IB version. Where the difference could
confuse her, add one short sentence. Standard wording for the notes:

| Topic | IB (keep) | HKDSE (mention) | Note to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Molar gas volume | STP = 273.15 K and 100 kPa; 22.7 dm³ mol⁻¹ | 常溫常壓 (room temperature and pressure): 24 dm³ mol⁻¹ | 「IB 用 STP（273.15 K、100 kPa），摩爾體積是 22.7 dm³ mol⁻¹。香港課本常用常溫常壓的 24 dm³ mol⁻¹，IB 考試不要用這個數值。」 |
| Amount | "amount of substance", symbol *n* | 摩爾數 | 「物質的量即香港課本所說的『摩爾數』。」 |
| Electron arrangement | s, p, d, f sublevels; 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ … | shells only: 2,8,8,1 | 「香港課本只寫 2,8,1。IB 要寫出亞層。」 |
| Bonding names | coordination bond; Lewis formula; London (dispersion) forces | 配位共價鍵 (dative covalent bond); 電子圖 (點叉圖); 范德華力 | Give the IB term; mention the HK one once. |
| Oxidation | oxidation state, written +2 | oxidation number 氧化數 | 「氧化態即香港課本所說的『氧化數』。」 |
| Organic names | IUPAC: halogenoalkane, alcohol, carboxylic acid | 鹵烷, 烷醇, 烷酸 (as names of the series) | Use 鹵烷、醇、羧酸. 烷醇 and 烷酸 are correct only for the saturated series. |
| Isomers | cis–trans isomers | 幾何異構 (C) / 順反異構 | Use 順反異構. |
| Level labels | SL, Additional HL (AHL) | 必修部分 / 選修部分 | Never map AHL onto 選修. Use 「HL 附加內容」. |
| Units | dm³, cm³, kPa, mol dm⁻³ | same, plus M for mol dm⁻³ | Never write M. |

### Phrasing to avoid

| Avoid | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 進行計算、作出比較、進行加熱 | 計算、比較、加熱 | Empty verbs make sentences long. |
| 被 … 所 … (heavy passive) | active sentence, or 由 … | Natural Chinese prefers the active. |
| 當 … 的時候 at every "when" | … 時, or two short sentences | Translationese. |
| 它們 for particles in every clause | repeat the noun, or drop the subject | Clearer for a learner. |
| 一個 before every noun | drop it unless counting | Translationese. |
| 的 chains (氯化鈉的晶格的結構的 …) | split the sentence | Hard to read. |
| 因此、故此、然而 in every sentence | 所以、但 | Plainer. |
| 物質的量濃度, 物質的量分數 | 摩爾濃度 | Mainland compound terms. |
| 原子量, 分子量 | 相對原子質量, 相對分子質量 | Old terms. |
| 克分子, 當量濃度 | 摩爾, 摩爾濃度 | Obsolete. |

chemistry teacher or a current HKDSE or IB Chinese textbook should confirm it.
